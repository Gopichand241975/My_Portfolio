Accepted at ICTCon 2026 Accepted & Appreciated

Human Detection for SAR: Onboard vs Edge vs Cloud Inference

Gopichand Sabbavarapu | Central Institute of Technology Kokrajhar

In Search and Rescue (SAR) drone missions, timely detection of humans in rough terrain can save lives. This research built and benchmarked a YOLOv8n-based human detection pipeline trained on the VisDrone dataset. The study conducts an in-depth empirical comparison evaluating latency, computing overhead, and power consumption across three architectures: Onboard UAV Inference, Ground Edge Gateway, and Centralized Cloud Inference.

🚁

Onboard UAV

Zero Network Latency

Processes video directly on drone hardware via ONNX Runtime; constrained by flight battery and payload compute.

📡

Edge Gateway

Optimal Balance

Streams raw video to local ground station via wireless relay, conserving drone power while keeping latency low.

☁️

Cloud Inference

High Compute Power

Allows large ensemble architectures, but requires steady cellular/satellite bandwidth and introduces transmission lag.
